# Practitioner register — EgD-REG-PRAC-001

Advisers who have signed up to run the EVEglyphDesign Global Compliance Framework kit with
their own clients under [EgD-LIC-PRAC-001](../PRACTITIONER-LICENCE.md).

Signing up is a public act: it says this person scores vendor proposals with a named,
versioned instrument rather than freehand. It is not a certification, an endorsement, or a
statement about the quality of anyone's work. EVEglyphDesign does not assess practitioners
and does not receive a fee from them.

**To be listed**, open a
[practitioner sign-up issue](https://github.com/EVEglyphDesign/enterprise-grade-criteria/issues/new?template=practitioner-signup.yml).
Entries are appended in the order received. To be removed, or to correct a row, open an
issue saying so — a removal is honoured without discussion.

Handles are recorded so that a piece of work can be shared across LinkedIn, X and YouTube
in one pass rather than reposted piecemeal, including through the SAPfans community. A
practitioner who asks to be listed without being tagged is listed without handles.

## What a listed practitioner is asked to do

1. Use the documents unmodified and name the instrument in client deliverables.
2. Report a defect in the method as a repository issue rather than patching it locally — a
   correction that stays in one firm's copy makes two practitioners' scores incomparable.
3. Keep client scores private. There is no shared score register, and there will not be one.
