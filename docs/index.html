The seven-point audit

An AI platform vendor claiming enterprise tier should publish its foundation-model roster, publish its alignment overlay, publish failure-mode disclosures at real cadence, provide substrate-selection controls, accept liability commensurate with the tier claim, publish an operator-reachable escalation path, and fund a red team on substrate honesty. The full statement is in the repository README.

The rubric runs at product tier, not at persons. It does not adjudicate individual founders, executives, or employees at any vendor.
